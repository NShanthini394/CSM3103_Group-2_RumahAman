Safety Scores

How is the safety score calculated?

Safety scores are generated by analysing area crime statistics, historical incident reports, and hotspot density. Each neighbourhood is assigned a score from 0 to 100 — the higher the number, the safer the area. Scores are updated periodically as new public data becomes available.

What does a score of 90+ mean?

A score of 90 and above indicates a very safe neighbourhood with low recorded crime rates. While no area can guarantee zero incidents, properties in this range show consistently lower crime trends compared to city averages.

How often are safety scores updated?

Safety scores are refreshed on a monthly basis as we ingest new data from official public datasets. For areas with sudden incidents, scores may be updated sooner to reflect current conditions.

Can I compare safety scores between two areas?

Yes. When you view a property listing, you can tap "Compare Area" to place two neighbourhoods side-by-side. The comparison shows their safety scores, crime category breakdowns, and nearby amenity ratings.

Property Search

Can I search houses near my current location?

Yes, RumahAman supports location-based property discovery. Simply allow location access in your browser or app and tap "Near Me" to see properties within your chosen radius, ranked by safety score.

Does RumahAman list both rental and sale properties?

Yes. You can filter listings by type — rent, buy, or both — using the filter toolbar in the property search page. Each listing clearly labels whether it is available for rent or purchase.

How do I save a property I like?

Tap the bookmark icon on any property card to save it to your Wishlist. You can access your saved properties anytime from your profile dashboard. A registered account is required to use the Wishlist feature.

Can I view crime hotspots on a map for a specific property?

Absolutely. Inside a property detail page, tap the "Crime Map" tab to load an interactive map overlay showing reported incident hotspots within a 2 km radius of the property.

Account & Privacy

Do I need an account to use RumahAman?

Basic property browsing and safety score viewing are available without an account. However, features like saving properties, generating detailed safety reports, and accessing personalised recommendations require a free registered account.

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email address. A password reset link will be sent to your inbox within a few minutes. The link expires after 30 minutes for security.

Is my personal data kept private?

Your personal data is never shared with or sold to third parties. RumahAman only collects information necessary to provide the service — such as your email and search preferences — and it is stored securely in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Once confirmed, all your data including saved properties and preferences will be permanently removed within 7 days. This action cannot be undone.

Data & Reports

Where does RumahAman's crime data come from?

All crime information is sourced from official public datasets released by relevant government authorities. We do not rely on user-submitted reports for our core safety scores, ensuring data reliability and consistency.

Can I download a safety report for an area?

Yes. Registered users can generate and download a PDF safety report for any area directly from the property detail page or the map view. The report includes a safety score breakdown, crime category analysis, and trend history.

How accurate is the crime data?

We use only officially published datasets, which represent reported crime. It is important to note that not all crimes are reported to authorities, so our data reflects recorded incidents rather than all possible incidents. We always recommend using our scores as one factor among many when making housing decisions.
