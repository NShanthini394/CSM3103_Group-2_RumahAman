Our Mission & Story

RumahAman started as a university project with a simple question — "How do you know if a neighbourhood is actually safe before you move in?" Most people rely on word of mouth or gut feeling, but that is not good enough when it comes to choosing a home.

We built RumahAman to bridge that gap. By combining publicly available crime data with an easy-to-use property search tool, we give house hunters a clear picture of the safety landscape around any listing — before they sign anything.

Our mission is simple: help people make smarter, safer housing decisions through data and transparency.

Why We Built This

Three real problems we saw — and set out to solve.

Lack of Safety Awareness

Most property listing platforms show price, size, and location — but say nothing about how safe the surrounding area actually is.

Data Is Hard to Find

Crime statistics are publicly available but scattered across different government portals and hard for the average person to interpret.

Families Deserve Better

Families with children, elderly relatives, or specific safety needs have no dedicated tool to factor crime risk into their housing search.
